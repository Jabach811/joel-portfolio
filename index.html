How it goes

Four steps. No runaround.

01

A 30-minute talk

About the business, the goals, and the parts that feel harder than they should. No pitch deck.

02

Assessment

I walk the whole customer path, from the first search to the follow-up call, and write down what's losing people.

03

Priorities and scope

We agree on the work with the most business value and a plan that fits the budget. Then I build it.

04

Launch and stay

Good systems need an owner after launch. I handle updates, the details, and the open work.
